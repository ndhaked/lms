How to add staff and choose what they can do?

Staff → Add staff → enter name, phone and email → tick one or more roles → Save. They get an email link to choose their own password. What each role may do is set under Roles & Permissions. If someone is locked out, use Reset access on their row to send a new link.

How to set up halls and floors?

Halls & Floors → Add hall → enter name, capacity, opening and closing time and the floor supervisor → Save. Seats are created inside a hall.

How to create seats and give one to a student?

Seats → Generate seats → choose the hall, seat type, prefix, start number and how many → Generate. To give a seat, press Allocate next to it and pick the student. Press Release when they leave. Switch between Seat map and Table views at the top.

How to create membership plans?

Membership Plans → Add plan → enter plan name, duration, price, registration fee, seat type, grace days and the shift start and end → Save. Plans can then be assigned to students.

How to register a new student?

Students → Register student → enter full name and phone (email, address, member type and an emergency contact are optional) → Save. Then open the student and press Assign membership.

How to give a student a membership?

Open the student and press Assign membership (or go to Memberships → Assign membership) → choose the plan, start date and any discount → Assign. The amount becomes a due until you record the payment.

How to renew a membership?

Memberships → find the student → Renew → choose the plan → Save. By default the new period starts after the old one ends. A student can also ask for a renewal from My Library; you approve it on the Memberships page.

How to record a payment or collect outstanding dues?

Payments → Record payment → choose what it applies to → enter amount, method, date and reference or UTR → Record payment. Part payments are fine; the balance stays in Outstanding Dues. Each payment has a Receipt, and a mistake can be fixed with Refund or Reverse.

A student says they paid. How do I confirm it?

Turn on student payment submissions in Settings. Students then use "I have paid" in My Library, and their details appear under Student Payments. Check the money arrived and confirm it, or reject it with a reason.

How to check students in and out?

Attendance → Check-in → search by name, phone or student ID → Check in. Only students with an active membership can check in. Press Check out when they leave. If someone forgot to check out, use Close visit, or Correct and give a reason.
